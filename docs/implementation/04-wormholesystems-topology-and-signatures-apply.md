## Galaxy decision boundary and freshness

Use the **current persisted** unit-03 policy (initially 60,000 ms; positive safe integer) under the canonical-map `mapDecisionBoundary` at final send and after any wait/retry. Report a separate `eligible_for_topology_apply` and blockers; never reuse unit 03's *full note-matching* `eligible_for_matching` as the topology precondition. `snapshot.fetchedAt` comes from a completed validated entire map/member fetch, finite/nonfuture and within the policy. No per-row `observedAt`, fabricated `active` state, Nexum cached system or preexisting signature-resource timestamp is required for *creation*. The deployed Nexum endpoint also checks `fetchedAt` within its own 10-minute server ceiling at validation, under map lock and before commit; do not mistake that looser cap for Galaxy's policy or a scan observation age. Persisted-policy corruption, disabled source sync, config/credential/map/source revision change, failed last source fetch, stale source or missing schema confirmation block **all** sends and deletion. Recheck snapshot revision/digest, source identity and map ID, policy revision and source age inside the map-decision boundary immediately before a PUT; hold exclusive Galaxy map write ownership through response/provenance update, without a SQLite transaction across HTTP. Policy setter returns busy/no-commit while this boundary is held, mode/config changes wait/serialize, and queued obsolete plans are invalidated.
